# How targets.json was built

Targets judged against the relevance criteria in specs.json from two fixed-template web searches per idea (2026-10-19), resolved through arXiv id_list or Crossref/arXiv title search. Excluded: papers named in result text without a link or identifier (e.g. Grinsztajn et al. 2022, Li and Talwalkar 2019), and 'Unbiased Evaluation of Deep Metric Learning Algorithms', which could not be resolved. 'You CAN Teach an Old Dog New Tricks!' is OpenReview-only and kept as a title-only target.
